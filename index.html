My 掌櫃 私隱政策

Privacy policy — My 掌櫃 (hk.MyCheungKwai.app)

生效日期 / Effective: 2026-08-31

1. 我們收集什麼 / What we collect

本 App 係本機個人記帳工具。記帳、帳戶、信用卡後四位、預算、分帳朋友姓名／電話、通知自動記帳內容，預設只存你部手機，唔會上載去開發者伺服器（我哋冇帳本雲端）。

This is a local personal ledger. Expenses, accounts, card last-4, budgets, split-friend names/numbers, and notification-capture text stay on your device by default. We do not operate a ledger server.

2. 通知存取 / Notification access

若你開啟「自動記帳」，App 會喺你確認披露對話框之後，讀取付款相關通知（例如 Wallet、PayMe、八達通、銀行）以便寫入本機帳本。系統雜訊、還卡等會過濾。通知紀錄最多約 50 筆、含敏感原文，唔會跟備份一齊還原，亦唔會上傳。

If you enable Auto-capture, after a prominent disclosure the app reads payment-related notifications to book local expenses. Noise and card repayments are skipped. The notification log (capped at about 50 rows) is sensitive, is not restored from backup, and is not uploaded.

3. 連絡人 / Contacts

只有你撳「從連絡人選擇」先會要求讀取連絡人，而且只用嚟填分帳朋友嘅姓名同電話。資料留喺裝置，亦唔會寫入雲端／本機備份檔。

Contacts are requested only when you tap “Pick from contacts”, and only to fill a split friend’s name and number. That data stays on device and is excluded from backups.

4. Google 雲端硬碟 / Google Drive

備份到 Drive 係你主動操作。範圍只係 drive.file（App 建立嘅備份 JSON），唔會讀你其他雲端檔案。預設明文上載；你可選擇用密碼加密備份（忘記密碼則無法還原該備份）。

Drive backup is user-initiated. Scope is drive.file only (the backup JSON this app creates). Default is plaintext; you may opt in to passphrase encryption (a forgotten passphrase cannot restore that backup).

5. 本機匯出 / Local export

CSV／JSON 匯出由你揀檔案位置。請自行保管備份。

CSV/JSON export is saved where you choose. You are responsible for those files.

6. App 鎖 / App lock

可選密碼或生物辨識。鎖係本機驗證，密碼雜湊唔會上雲。

Optional password or biometrics. Unlock is verified on-device; the password hash is not uploaded.

7. 唔會做嘅事 / What we do not do

  • 唔賣數據、唔做廣告追蹤 / No sale of data, no ad tracking
  • 唔要求你開戶去我哋伺服器 / No developer-operated account for the ledger

8. 聯絡 / Contact

請用 Google Play 上架資料嘅開發者電郵。 / Use the developer email on the Play listing.
