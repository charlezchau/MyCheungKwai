My 掌櫃 私隱政策

Privacy policy — My 掌櫃 (hk.MyCheungKwai.app)

生效日期 / Effective: 2026-08-27

1. 我們收集什麼 / What we collect

本 App 係本機個人記帳工具。記帳、帳戶、信用卡後四位、預算、分帳朋友姓名／電話、通知自動記帳內容，預設只存你部手機，唔會上載去我哋伺服器（我哋冇帳本雲端）。

This is a local personal ledger. Expenses, accounts, card last-4, budgets, split-friend names/numbers, and notification-capture text stay on your device by default. We do not operate a ledger server.

2. 通知存取 / Notification access

若你開啟「自動記帳」，App 會讀取付款相關通知（例如 Wallet、PayMe、八達通、銀行）以便寫入本機帳本。系統雜訊、還卡等會過濾。通知紀錄唔會跟備份一齊還原，亦唔會上傳。

If you enable Auto-capture, the app reads payment-related notifications to book local expenses. Noise and card repayments are skipped. The notification log is not restored from backup and is not uploaded.

3. 連絡人 / Contacts

只有你撳「從連絡人選擇」先會要求讀取連絡人，而且只用嚟填分帳朋友嘅姓名同電話，資料留喺裝置。

Contacts are requested only when you tap “Pick from contacts”, and only to fill a split friend’s name and number on device.

4. Google 雲端硬碟 / Google Drive

備份到 Drive 係你主動操作。範圍只係 drive.file（App 建立嘅備份 JSON），唔會讀你其他雲端檔案。

Drive backup is user-initiated. Scope is drive.file only (the backup JSON this app creates).

5. 本機匯出 / Local export

CSV／JSON 匯出由你揀檔案位置。請自行保管備份。

CSV/JSON export is saved where you choose. You are responsible for those files.

6. 唔會做嘅事 / What we do not do

  • 唔賣數據、唔做廣告追蹤 / No sale of data, no ad tracking
  • 唔要求你開戶去我哋伺服器 / No developer-operated account for the ledger

7. 聯絡 / Contact

請用 Google Play 上架資料嘅開發者電郵。 / Use the developer email on the Play listing.
